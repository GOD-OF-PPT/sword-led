## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Explicit approval and bounded expedition slice
The new sword-expedition rules SHALL remain unimplemented until the user approves the complete v0.9 proposal. The initial scope SHALL be one approximately ninety-second segment with a forge, main trial, at most one tool cache and that tool's first actual use in a fixed epilogue. Ninety seconds SHALL be a pacing hypothesis rather than a real-time deadline, measured result or acceptance claim. This slice SHALL NOT be labeled a complete twelve-chapter game or evidence of human enjoyment.

#### Scenario: Specification passes before approval
- **WHEN** this proposal and its strict validation are complete but no specific user approval has arrived
- **THEN** no v6 production rules, SDK, codec or new-rule page is implemented, while separately authorized current-version visual and causal-feedback work can continue

#### Scenario: Bounded slice delivery
- **WHEN** the first expedition segment is delivered after approval
- **THEN** its actual forge, trial, reward use and failure boundaries are labeled separately from any future full campaign or human fun judgment

### Requirement: Isolated v6 identity and preserved legacy meaning
The new mode SHALL use forge-v6, save schema6, independent sword-led.save.v6 and sword-led.save.backup.v6 keys, an independent official SDK and an explicit v6 entry. V5 and all earlier interpreters, SDKs, saves, collection origins and art originals SHALL retain their meaning. The initial slice SHALL NOT migrate old coins, qualifications, weapons, tools or power and SHALL NOT automatically replace the current default page.

#### Scenario: Existing v5 run remains available
- **WHEN** the user opens or continues a valid v5 run after v6 is added
- **THEN** v5 interprets its original journal, purse, prices and twelve-day campaign without conversion or v6 state writes

### Requirement: Finite forging and declared randomness
Each fresh blank SHALL start intact at strength0 and hammer count0, with one school chosen before its first hit and locked afterward. Each ordinary or technique hit SHALL count once toward a six-hit limit. Each ordinary hit SHALL consume one independent1000-ticket draw; the first three hits SHALL have zero crack probability, later intact hits200 tickets and later cracked hits350 tickets. Non-cracks SHALL add1 strength; a first crack SHALL only subtract1 strength with a floor of0 and mark cracked; a second crack SHALL break the blade. The slice SHALL NOT add a separate great-hit tier or multiple simultaneous heat, timing and material axes.

#### Scenario: Guaranteed opening and risky fourth hit
- **WHEN** an intact blank receives three ordinary hits and then an ordinary fourth hit
- **THEN** the first three hits produce strength3 without cracks and advance three draws, and only the fourth draw can trigger the declared20percent first-crack outcome

#### Scenario: Damage and hit cap
- **WHEN** a first crack occurs, a cracked blade cracks again, or a blade reaches six hits
- **THEN** the first crack subtracts1 without an additional success gain, the second crack removes the blade from usable supply, and a seventh hit is rejected without a draw or mutation

### Requirement: One finite next-hit technique per forge
The main forge and fixed epilogue forge SHALL each start with three fresh blanks and one technique charge shared across those blanks. An armed next-hit intent SHALL be cancellable without spending or random draws and bound to the current run, revision, generation, piece and school. Only an accepted valid technique hit SHALL consume the charge. Switching pieces, sealing, leaving, pause, backgrounding or stale identity SHALL cancel unaccepted intent without refunding accepted consumption.

#### Scenario: Cancel and later legitimate use
- **WHEN** the player arms then cancels a technique, and later arms and accepts a valid hit
- **THEN** cancellation changes no charge, strength or RNG, while the accepted hit spends exactly one charge and increments the hammer count once

#### Scenario: Switching blanks does not refill
- **WHEN** a technique was consumed on one blade and the player selects another unused blank in the same forge
- **THEN** the new blank can choose its school but the shared technique charge remains0 and no old intent follows it

### Requirement: Distinct steady and fissure schools
Steady-line SHALL make the next accepted technique hit add1 strength without a new crack and SHALL NOT repair existing cracks. Fissure SHALL require an intact blade, add3 strength, create a deliberate cracked fissure mark and consume no ordinary random draw. Only an actually marked fissure blade SHALL gain2 trial damage, ignore armor and lose2 strength after each strike with a floor of0. Ordinary accidental cracks SHALL NOT grant those benefits. Cracked blades SHALL be ineligible for fissure technique and for cutting the rope cache.

#### Scenario: Steady preserves only this hit
- **WHEN** steady-line is used on an intact or already cracked blade
- **THEN** strength increases1 without a new crack, the prior crack status remains, and a future ordinary hit still uses the declared normal risk

#### Scenario: Deliberate fissure has a cost
- **WHEN** an intact strength3 blade uses fissure and later strikes an armored target
- **THEN** it becomes marked cracked strength6, its strike computes8 damage before any opening bonus and ignores armor, then its strength falls to4

### Requirement: Sealed blades and bounded recovery attempts
Sealing SHALL require a usable blade with at least one accepted hit and SHALL lock its forging facts for trial. A trial SHALL use that one piece throughout. Failed, broken or abandoned blades SHALL retain readable records but SHALL NOT return as fresh blanks or retry the same main gate. A new main attempt SHALL require a remaining unused blank and SHALL reset the main target to full health without carrying partial damage. Exhausting all three main blanks before clearing SHALL produce an explicit segment failure and cancellable restart confirmation.

#### Scenario: Weak blade failure can change the next plan
- **WHEN** a strength2 blade spends its three turns without clearing the gate
- **THEN** its actual armor and damage history is shown, it cannot resubmit, and the player can use an unused blank for a stronger blade or a different still-available technique without a refund

### Requirement: Three-turn trial with explicit damage order
The main wooden guard SHALL start at health6 and armor2 with three action turns. A direct ordinary strike SHALL deal max of0 and strength minus armor. Guard-opening SHALL spend one turn to store one nonstacking opening that makes only the next strike ignore armor and add2 damage. A fissure strike SHALL use its pre-wear strength plus2, ignore armor and also gain an available opening bonus, then lose2 strength. Every accepted strike or guard SHALL spend exactly one turn; a zero-damage strike SHALL still spend a turn. After each action, target defeat SHALL be resolved before turn exhaustion failure.

#### Scenario: Steady route uses positioning
- **WHEN** a strength4 intact blade guards then strikes the main target
- **THEN** the strike deals6, the main gate clears in two turns and one turn remains, with the opening consumed

#### Scenario: Final-turn victory and unstacked guards
- **WHEN** an action defeats the target on the final turn, or the player guards repeatedly
- **THEN** final-turn defeat of the target is success, and each guard spends a turn but stores at most one opening rather than stacking damage bonuses

### Requirement: Once-only real gate reward
Clearing the main guard SHALL visibly open the road and allow the escort to pass, and SHALL award15 expedition coins exactly once for that run and gate. Payment SHALL NOT be sword-strength sale value or v5 purse income. Repeated commands, restore, replay or revisit SHALL NOT duplicate it. Trial failure SHALL award no gate money.

#### Scenario: Restore an already paid gate
- **WHEN** the player restores a run whose main gate was cleared and paid
- **THEN** the road and actual15-coin receipt remain factual and no old action, animation or repeated command pays again

### Requirement: Competing bonus targets and one tool
After clearing, the player SHALL enter a separate cache phase with the opening consumed by the gate-clearing strike and no temporary opening bonus. That phase SHALL accept only a direct cache-target attempt or leave, and SHALL reject guard-opening, another main-target strike or changing blades without a turn or state change. The player SHALL be able to leave or spend an available trial turn on at most one bonus cache. The rope SHALL require an intact strength of at least5 and award Rebound Tooth. The iron latch SHALL have armor2 and require at least7 actual direct strike damage with the same fissure and post-strike wear rules but without a guard-opening bonus, awarding Furnace Nail. An ineligible attempt SHALL be rejected with explanation and no turn, wear, charge or reward change. Successful acquisition SHALL close the segment's tool slot and prevent taking both caches. The safe strength4 steady and strength6 fissure main-gate routes SHALL NOT automatically qualify for their bonus caches. The preview SHALL distinguish the20percent intact ordinary hit before a technique from the35percent second-crack risk after fissure.

#### Scenario: Different swords earn tools after an actual risk
- **WHEN** a fourth ordinary hit succeeds from strength3 at a declared20percent crack risk, and the fifth hit is then steady-line or fissure
- **THEN** steady-line makes intact strength5 that can guard-strike for7 and cut the rope, while fissure makes strength7 that strikes the main guard for9, wears to5, strikes the latch for7 and wears to3 while gaining Furnace Nail

#### Scenario: Safe main route can leave without a tool
- **WHEN** the player takes the safe fourth-hit technique route and clears the main gate with intact strength4 or fissure strength4 after its strike
- **THEN** the15-coin gate payment is retained, the rope5 or latch7 condition remains unmet, and the UI offers leaving without granting a free cache

#### Scenario: No post-gate guard bypass
- **WHEN** a safe fissure strength6 blade clears the main target for8, wears to4 and has two turns remaining
- **THEN** guard-opening is rejected in the cache phase without spending a turn, and the direct latch damage remains6 rather than gaining an opening bonus to bypass the7 requirement

#### Scenario: No misleading rope or double reward
- **WHEN** a cracked blade tries the rope or the player tries another cache after acquiring one tool
- **THEN** the action refuses without economic mutation or extra turn cost and no second tool is granted

### Requirement: Tool effect demonstrated in a fixed epilogue
A tool acquisition SHALL enter one fixed epilogue forge and trial with one fresh three-blank supply and one technique charge. It SHALL NOT reopen the main gate, pay another gate fee, add another tool cache or transfer its fresh supply backward. During epilogue forging the player SHALL be able to choose remaining unused blanks without refunding consumed charges; exhausting those blanks SHALL end practice as incomplete. After a blade is submitted, the epilogue SHALL have only that one three-turn trial: exhaustion without its branch goal SHALL enter recap without resetting targets, turns, opening, follow-up or tool-trigger flags for another attempt. Rebound Tooth SHALL store positive overkill from one guard-opening strike as one optional target-selected follow-up; follow-up damage SHALL use only the stored amount, spend one turn, and SHALL NOT recursively create follow-ups. Furnace Nail SHALL restore one charge up to1 only on the epilogue's first defeat of its armored primary target, then allow exactly one return to forging another unused blank to use that charge, with no new trial or resupply. If no blank remains, the recharge SHALL be recorded but subsequent use SHALL remain unproven. Practice failure SHALL preserve the main-gate result, payment and acquired tool and SHALL NOT be labeled a successful tool demonstration.

#### Scenario: Actual rebound branch
- **WHEN** an intact strength4 blade guards then deals6 to an epilogue primary target of health2 and armor2
- **THEN** overkill4 is stored, and a player-selected follow-up can spend the third turn to defeat the health4 unarmored secondary target without adding blade strength again

#### Scenario: Actual furnace recharge branch
- **WHEN** the player spends the epilogue charge on fissure, defeats its health2 armored primary target and then forges another fresh blank
- **THEN** the first armored defeat restores charge1 once and a subsequent accepted fissure consumes it to0 while creating the second blade's real strength3 crack

#### Scenario: Failed epilogue does not reset into free retries
- **WHEN** an intact strength3 rebound blade guards, deals5 to the health2 primary target, follows up for3 against the health4 secondary target and exhausts its third turn
- **THEN** the secondary remains at health1 and practice ends incomplete in recap with the main-gate payment and tool retained, without fresh targets, turns, charge, supply or another trial

### Requirement: Restart, replay and presentation ownership
The mode SHALL validate unknown saved JSON by bounded structure and full deterministic journal replay, preserve independent main and backup sources, and retain a single active writer with current command identities. Restart SHALL explicitly create a new run with main supply3, charge1, coins0, no tool and unpaid gate; cancellation SHALL preserve the existing run. Accepted results SHALL belong to the core, with the renderer consuming events only. Pause, background, reduced motion or restoration SHALL NOT replay stale inputs, trial attacks, payments or tool grants.

#### Scenario: Cancel restart and cancel visuals
- **WHEN** the player cancels a restart confirmation or pauses after an accepted trial action
- **THEN** restart cancellation keeps the existing run unchanged and visual cancellation preserves exactly the accepted damage, turn and reward state without a new action

### Requirement: Honest verification and experiential scope
The change SHALL distinguish proposal arithmetic, actual SDK replay, legitimate restored boundaries, fresh UI play and human feedback. Necessary finite rules, storage, build and one-PC-browser checks SHALL support the slice, including both schools, failure adjustment, tool use and two desktop layouts. It SHALL NOT require unrelated v5 full-matrix or1000-seed reruns solely to add this mode. Human decisions and repeated-input boredom SHALL be recorded separately from technical success; no passing count, mockup or proposal approval SHALL be presented as proof that fun has been achieved.

#### Scenario: Arithmetic examples are not executions
- **WHEN** the documents show strength4 guard plus strike6 or strength6 fissure strike8
- **THEN** those examples are labeled unexecuted contract arithmetic until separately consumed by actual approved code and UI
