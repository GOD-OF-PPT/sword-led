# B2 青釉断金：新主界面方向稿

用途：v0.3提案的单张UI方向图，不是运行素材或已实现页面。

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original PC browser forging game main gameplay screen, landscape 16:9, high fidelity art direction concept.
Primary request: Redesign a Chinese hammer-and-sword forging game so it feels like a distinctive handcrafted game rather than a generic medieval simulator or beige admin dashboard. The player shapes a memorable individual sword, chooses strike locations along its blade, and forges a visible glowing pattern. Create one coherent in-game screen.
Scene/backdrop: An intimate original artisan forge with indigo glazed ceramic tiles, pale ash timber, charcoal ink linework, tiny copper furnace and a hanging paper commission at the side. A few repaired ceramic surfaces have narrow gold seams that echo the sword's forged pattern. No photorealism and no generic European castle window scene.
Subject: A large unfinished single-edged sword laid horizontally across a modest dark anvil/workbench, blade facing camera, five clear axial sections and two subtle lanes of ten selectable forging marks. The sword and hammer interaction occupy the central 55 percent of the screen. A bold warm orange hot edge, cool blue spine, a branching fine gold forging trail, and an outlined active strike location with a nearby hammer. Materials have visibly different silhouettes and surface patterns.
Style/medium: confident hand-painted 2D game art, precise woodcut-like contour lines, flat gouache color masses, restrained tactile texture, irregular silhouettes and expressive tiny original merchant portrait. Readable at a glance; bespoke, playful, sophisticated, warm. Absolutely not a photo collage, 3D render, comic panel, or website landing page.
Composition/framing: Full 16:9 gameplay UI. Narrow top strip for round 2/12, quota 96/150, three seals and coins/scrap. Right 24 percent is a cream paper order with one courier portrait, required sharpness/toughness/balance symbols, and a small sword test sketch. Left 10 percent shows three small distinct incoming blanks vertically. Bottom band is a compact set of three illustrated hammer buttons and water/oil quench bowls, remaining strikes 3/6, heat and stress plus a concise timing arc directly next to the active forge mark. The blade remains completely unobscured by large tooltips. Clear delivery and salvage buttons. No duplicated panels or sprawling numeric dashboard.
Text: Use short legible Chinese labels sparingly: "锤下求生", "第2轮", "锋", "韧", "衡", "落锤", "淬火", "试剑", "交货", "回收". Avoid long generated paragraphs. Other stats can use simple short numbers and symbols.
Color palette: mineral indigo #244956, celadon #84A49A, ivory paper #F3E7CE, soot #202A2B, hot cinnabar #D95D35, small restrained gold #D6AD57. Preserve contrast without flooding the entire screen beige or black.
Constraints: Entirely original designs; do not copy any existing game's UI, character, logo, sword, or art. No Steam branding. This is a concept of a playable interface with visible affordances, not a mock browser frame. No panels floating in 3D, no circular holograms, no huge giant anvil hiding the sword, no tiny iron brick as the central hero.
