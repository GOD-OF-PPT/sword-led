# D 行脚灯炉：新主界面方向稿

用途：v0.3提案的单张UI方向图，与B2承载同一玩法，不是运行素材或已实现页面。

Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: original PC browser forging game main gameplay screen, landscape 16:9, high fidelity alternative art direction.
Primary request: An original Chinese-language sword-forging game with a striking, expressive hand-drawn graphic identity. Make it feel like playing with a miniature traveling forge lit by a lantern at dusk. Its central interaction is choosing strike marks along a large visible blade, building a meaningful forging pattern, and testing the resulting sword for a customer's commission.
Scene/backdrop: A compact traveling smith's fold-out wooden forge cart in a quiet dusk campsite. Carved amber timber, folded ink-blue fabric canopy, glowing paper lantern, small contained furnace. The scene is close and intimate, not an open-world landscape. Silhouettes have strong deliberate shape language and lively cut-paper layering.
Subject: A long, beautifully silhouetted unfinished curved sword displayed horizontally on the folding workbench. Five axial sword sections, two lanes of ten small embedded strike marks, a few linked warm luminous forging scars along the blade, active mark clearly indicated. Small hammer poised at active point. The sword is the hero of the screen, not the background or an oversized anvil. A small cheeky original courier illustration appears on the commission tag.
Style/medium: Premium 2D indie game art, hand-cut linocut prints and layered painted paper, crisp chunky angular forms, confident dark contours, expressive proportions, limited rich colors, no photorealism or 3D rendering. Atmospheric but readable. Entirely original visual vocabulary.
Composition/framing: Full 16:9 game screen. Narrow top bar showing round 2/12, quota 96/150, three seals, coins and scrap. Three distinct material silhouettes in a slim tray at left. Sword/forge occupies central 55 percent. Right 24 percent is a hanging cream commission tag with portrait and three distinct icons for sharpness/toughness/balance, plus a tiny sword-test diagram. Compact bottom controls: three illustrated hammer choices, water and oil quench choices, strikes 3/6, heat and stress. Short timing arc near the selected strike mark. Clear deliver and salvage buttons. Readable margins and clickable controls; avoid cramped text or multi-column dashboard clutter.
Text: Short Chinese labels only where useful: "锤下求生", "第2轮", "锋", "韧", "衡", "落锤", "淬火", "试剑", "交货", "回收". No long paragraphs.
Color palette: midnight plum #30283C, ink blue #314C67, warm lantern apricot #F4B55F, terracotta #B85446, pale paper #F3E3BE, small mint #94C1AB. Warm lighting on the interaction area, calm cool perimeter, strong legible text contrast.
Constraints: Same mechanics and information density as the B2 concept. No existing game branding, copied characters, ornate fantasy HUD, browser mockup frame, neon hologram, generic beige dashboard, giant iron brick, or marketing splash screen. Use original cart, tools, materials and weapon design.
